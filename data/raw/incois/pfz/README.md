Brief description:
This raw file contains the official INCOIS Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) advisory for the North Tamil Nadu coastal sector (SEC007), encompassing Chennai, Pulicat, Ennore, Kasikoilkuppam, Ayothikuppam, Srinivasapuram, Palavakkam, Kovalam, and other fishing landing centres along the northern Tamil Nadu coastline in the Bay of Bengal. The dataset includes 90 landing centre records with geographic coordinates (Latitude, Longitude in DMS/DD), bearing angle in degrees, directional heading, distance from coast (km), and depth range (metres) indicating prospective marine fishing zones derived from satellite Sea Surface Temperature (SST) and Ocean Colour (Chlorophyll) data.
